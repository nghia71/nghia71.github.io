# Our results at the Purple Comet! Math Meet

MCC has taken part in the [Purple Comet! Math Meet](https://purplecomet.org) every April for ten years, under the club's own name and, in earlier years, as the Math & Chess Club in Ottawa.

## 2026 — three teams first in the world

Out of 4,804 teams from 86 countries: MS ONE and MS TWO each scored a perfect 20/20 and HS ONE a perfect 30/30, all three tying for World Champion; MS THREE took an Honorable Mention at 19/20.
