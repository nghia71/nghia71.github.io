# T4 — Team Track Level 4

**The capstone.** T4 is for students who can already solve olympiad problems and now want mastery: advanced technique, solutions written to a standard that would be graded without complaint, and the stamina to hold concentration for hours.

## Who it is for

* Students who have completed T3 successfully.
* Students capable of solving challenging olympiad problems independently.
* Students aiming at national olympiads, team selection exams, or the IMO.
* Students who can commit **7–8 hours a week**.

## Curriculum

T4 continues with the club's textbook **[Learning Problem Solving VII — From Insight to Mastery](./books.md)** — the same 55 chapters as T3, on a **second pass at mastery level**, taking the hardest material in each section.

What T4 emphasises:

* Mastery of the classical olympiad topics: geometry, algebra, number theory, combinatorics.
* Advanced tools and theorems, and the tricks that only show up at this level.
* Writing with precision, for formal grading.
* Strategic thinking across a whole problem set, not one problem at a time.
* Endurance and time management in multi-hour contests.

Assignments are fewer but deeper, designed to simulate olympiad exam conditions.

## The two-week cycle

**Week 1** — Study the chapter: theory, then the worked examples. Work the first half of the team assignment and submit it by Sunday. From the second cycle onward: official solutions to the previous assignment on Monday, grading and feedback from the previous test Monday–Thursday, selected teams preparing Friday to present on Sunday.

**Week 2** — Work the second half of the assignment and submit by Sunday. Official solutions Monday; review them as a team. **Sunday: the team test.**

## Tests

Each chapter has its own test: three problems on the chapter just studied, three from the other areas.

| Band | Problems | Points each | Questions per problem |
|---|---|---|---|
| **E — Easy** | 2 | 10 | 1 |
| **M — Medium** | 2 | 15 | 2 |
| **H — Hard** | 2 | 25 | 3 |

**Total 100 points. The pass mark is 51. Duration: 180 minutes.**

At this level the paper is as much a test of pacing as of mathematics. Deciding which problem to open, when to abandon a line, and how long to spend writing up is part of what is being examined.

Every problem is drawn from an international competition and rewritten for clarity, with detailed step-by-step solutions published for grading.

Teams choose their testing pace — weekly, every two weeks, or one test per chapter. See [Programs](./programs.md).

## Submitting and grading

One PDF per submission, through the form. No email, no photographs, no multiple files; submissions in the wrong format lose points.

Tests are graded with full olympiad-style feedback. Selected teams present live on Sunday, or send a recording. Results and individual evaluations are published on the site.

## What you should be able to do by the end

* Solve olympiad-level problems across all four major areas.
* Use advanced theorems, methods, and strategies with confidence.
* Write formal solutions at national and international olympiad standard.
* Manage time and stamina across a long contest.
* Work strategically with a teammate under exam conditions.

Graduates of T4 are prepared for national olympiads, team selection tests, and international contests including the IMO.
