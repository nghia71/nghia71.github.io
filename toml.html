TOML

The Online Mathematics Laboratory — now the club's Coding Programme

New for 2026-2027. TOML has been rewritten around a single, focused programme: coding practice for Middle School and High School students, with a coding competition folded into the club's weekly Sunday session. The earlier, broader "explore any topic computationally" course has been retired.

Who it's for

Every MCC student, Middle School (MS) and High School (HS) alike. There is no separate registration — if you're in the club, you're in the coding programme.

How it works

  • Three problems a week, at your level. Problems are posted on this page and graded by difficulty, the same way the math tests are.
  • Solve and submit online. Submissions go through the same kind of online form used for math assignments — no email, no photographs.
  • Feedback on every problem. Each submission gets a comment back, not just a score.

The coding competition

A coding competition is one of the three activities that rotate through the club's weekly Sunday session — alongside a lecture on a math theory or topic, and a lecture on recent test solutions. See Organization and Programs for how the rotation works and who runs it.
