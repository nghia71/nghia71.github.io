# Purple Comet training: results

Each mock's results appear here when its window closes: for each team, the number of correct answers out of 20 (Middle School) or 30 (High School), and a running total over all mocks. Teams are shown by team name only.

A result marked *not verified* was submitted, but not every team member was in the Zoom attendance for the whole mock and on camera at the start and the end (see the [rules](/purple-comet-training#rules)).
