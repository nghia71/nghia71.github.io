# Purple Comet training

From January to March 2027 the club trains for the [Purple Comet! Math Meet](https://purplecomet.org), which runs from **6 to 15 April 2027**. Every two weeks on a Sunday, teams sit a past Purple Comet paper under real contest conditions, then study the solutions. The training replaces the TOML coding contest, which ended with Test 5 on 29 November 2026.

<div class="note" markdown="1">
**Sign-up opens on 14 December 2026**, the day the first-semester results are published, and **closes on 8 January 2027**. The training is **opt-in**: only students who sign up take part, in the training and in the April contest. Look at the [first-semester results](/current-standings) before you choose your teammates.
</div>

## How a mock works

* **The paper is a real past Purple Comet paper**, Middle School (20 problems) or High School (30 problems).
* **Every answer is a whole number** (0, 1, 2, …). Teams submit **answers only**: no working, no solutions, no code.
* **One submission per team**, on one answer form for the whole paper, sent by the team's chosen member before the time is up. **The first submission counts**; later ones are ignored.
* **Scoring:** 1 point per correct answer, out of 20 (MS) or 30 (HS). Results appear on the [results page](/purple-comet-training/results) when the window closes.
* **After each mock**, the [solutions](/purple-comet-training/solutions) are published, and the paper and answer key appear on the [mocks page](/purple-comet-training/mocks).

## Rules

* **Zoom with video.** Every team member joins the club Zoom for the whole mock, with the **camera on, showing the student and their paper**. A phone can join as a second camera pointed at the desk. Each team works in its own breakout room.
* **Calculators and computers are allowed for calculations**, as in the real contest. **No AI of any kind** (ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Claude, …): not to solve, explain, check or interpret a problem. This is Purple Comet's own rule, and a team that breaks it in April is disqualified.
* **No help from anyone outside the team** during the mock.
* A result counts as **verified** when every team member is in the Zoom attendance for the whole mock and on camera at the start and the end. Other results are shown but marked *not verified*.

## Teams

* **Students form their own teams of up to 6.** Teams may mix countries: Purple Comet no longer requires team members to live near each other.
* **Middle School or High School.** A Middle School team has only middle-school students (Purple Comet: not yet finished grade 8, or the local equivalent, and under 16). **A high-school student cannot be on a Middle School team.** Any team with a high-school student is a High School team; middle-school students may join a High School team.
* **Signing up.** Each student fills in the sign-up form separately, with their Student ID, the team's name, their teammates' Student IDs, their school level and time zone. A team is confirmed when every member has named the same team. A student without a team can ask to be placed on one, and Teacher Nghia places them by division and time zone.
* **The teams train together in every mock** and enter Purple Comet together in April. The club enters all its teams in the *Mixed* category, with Teacher Nghia as supervisor.

## In April

Each team sits Purple Comet 2027 once, in a slot of its choice between 6 and 15 April, on the same Zoom set-up as the mocks. The slots are agreed in March.

Học sinh chưa có đội có thể xin được xếp đội; Thầy Nghĩa sẽ xếp theo cấp học và múi giờ. Các đội luyện cùng nhau trong mọi buổi và thi Purple Comet cùng nhau vào tháng 4, ở hạng mục *Mixed*, Thầy Nghĩa là người giám sát.
