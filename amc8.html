Sign in with your Student ID and PIN (the same as on the Lessons and TOML pages). A test opens only on its booked day. Each test can be taken once.

How a test works

  1. Start. The paper loads, then you have 1 minute to read the instructions and tick the honesty pledge. The 40-minute timer starts when you press Begin, or after that minute. If your connection is slow, the timer waits until the paper is fully shown (at most 3 minutes after Start).
  2. Test, 40 minutes. 25 multiple-choice questions on this page; click your answer under each question. Every click is saved at once. Press Submit answers when done; at 40 minutes the test is submitted automatically, even if you closed the page.
  3. Self-evaluation, up to 25 minutes (optional). For each question click your second choice, how sure you were, what you did, which idea you used and what was hard. It does not change your score.
  4. Results. Score, correct answers, solutions, analysis and feedback on your own choices. Use Download PDF to keep a copy. You can come back to your results at any time.

Rules: work alone; no calculator, books, notes, other people, AI or online tools. Scratch paper, pencil, ruler and compass are allowed. Do not share the problems or answers.
